Common Incident ManagementControl Narrative

Defines the governance, control design and operational procedures supporting Incident Management controls under the Computer Operations domain. The process ensures that incidents are logged, categorised, prioritised, investigated, escalated, resolved, communicated and closed in a timely manner, with sufficient evidence retained to support ICFR, SOX readiness and audit requirements.

Process model

The incident lifecycle

The common Incident Management process is operated as an enterprise IT service-management process and applies across IT services rather than to a single application. Select a stage for the detail.

Categorisation and prioritisation criteria determine the applicable incident path, support assignment and escalation requirements. Incidents requiring a technology change route to Change Management, incidents linked to an existing problem route to Problem Management, and potential disaster events are considered for ITSCM and Disaster Recovery invocation.
Severity drives everything

Five severity classifications

Incident resolution expectations are aligned to severity and business impact. The severity selected determines the coverage window, the resolution target and the escalation route. Select a severity for the detail.

Escalation matrix

Who is engaged, and when

The escalation matrix objective is to maintain accurate and current escalation contact details for Wipro and RSA stakeholders, enabling reachability and swift escalation during urgent or critical requirements.

Supporting components

Technology landscape

Application or platform monitoring tools may generate events or incident inputs, but their application-specific configuration and operation are outside this common process.

From 1 July 2026, accountability for Incident Management and Major Incident Management transfers from the earlier Wipro-managed service model to Intact under the revised operating model. Operational support continues to involve internal teams and third-party resources where applicable. P1 and P2 incidents follow the Major Incident Management route and P3 and P4 incidents follow the standard Incident Management route. ServiceNow supports the common incident-management process. As part of SOX Test of Design attribute testing, evidence will be obtained to support the final post-transition control owner, control operator, formal ownership RACI, control handover testing and control revalidation.
